The healing loop

Six stages, fully autonomous, each one gated by the last. The same bar you hold human engineers to — evidence first, ship second.

  1. Watchdog

    Tails logs and health checks on your live service. Catches error spikes and crashes with full tracebacks.

  2. Diagnostician

    Reads the traceback against your indexed source and pins the root cause — file, line, and the input that triggers it.

  3. Reproducer

    The step nobody else does. Writes a failing test in an ephemeral sandbox that proves the bug. If it can't prove it, the pipeline stops.

  4. Fixer

    Patches until the reproduction test passes and your full suite stays green — verified in sandboxes, never on prod.

  5. Gate

    Optional human approval on the verified diff — or fully autonomous once you trust it. Autonomy is a dial, not a switch.

  6. Canary

    Ships to a slice of live traffic, watches the error rate, auto-promotes or rolls back. The regression test stays in your repo forever.
