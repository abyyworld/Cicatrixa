The procedure

Five operations, performed autonomously, recorded on the chart.

01Admission Connect GitHub in one click, pick a repo — or compose a project from several: frontend, backend, workers. Each becomes its own service.
02Diagnosis The AI reads the repository like a chart: framework, entry point, ports. No Dockerfile? It writes one.
03Operation Containers are built and started on a sealed internal network — no port numbers, no conflicts, ever. Your subdomain routes itself.
04Observation The deploy is probed and smoke-tested; the AI signs off on the result in writing. Failures are diagnosed from the logs, patched, and retried.
05Recovery Watchdogs keep vigil: pushes redeploy in seconds, dead containers are revived, chronic failures are rebuilt from source. The old version serves until the new one proves healthy.

Kept under observation

Three watchdogs, one promise: you find out it broke after it's fixed.

Push reflex

GitHub webhooks land the moment you push; the changed service — only that one — rebuilds and swaps in blue/green.

Pulse check

Every minute, every container. Flatline → restart. Restart twice and still dying → full rebuild from source.

Metabolic limits

RAM and storage are metered per account with hard caps per container. One tenant can never starve another.
